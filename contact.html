Frequently asked questions

Learn how Stackly helps businesses protect important data, prepare for unexpected incidents, and recover with confidence.

What is a backup and recovery solution? add

It helps create protected copies of important business data and restore that data after accidental deletion, system failure, or other disruptions.

How often should my business back up its data? add

The schedule depends on how frequently your data changes and how much information your business can afford to lose. Critical systems may require more frequent backups.

How can I prepare for ransomware or data loss? add

Maintain protected backup copies, restrict access, regularly test restoration procedures, and keep an incident recovery plan ready.

Can I restore data after a system failure? add

Recovery depends on the availability and integrity of your backups, the affected systems, and your recovery plan. Regular restore tests help identify issues early.

How do I choose the right backup plan? add

Consider your data volume, security requirements, recovery time objectives, retention needs, and budget. Stackly can help you discuss the requirements for your business.
